Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What data I collect

When you use the contact form on this site, the following data is collected: your name, email address, and message. This data is processed by Formspree (a third-party service) and forwarded to my personal inbox. I collect no other personal data myself; see section 3 for the font service the pages load. This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics scripts.

2. How I use it

Your contact submission is used solely to respond to your inquiry. It is not shared with any third party beyond Formspree's own processing, is not used for marketing, and is not sold. Formspree may retain a copy of the submission; refer to their privacy policy for details.

3. Cookies, fonts & analytics

This site does not use first-party cookies or analytics trackers. Formspree may set its own cookies during form submission; see the Formspree privacy policy for details.

The pages use the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information as part of that request. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ and Google's privacy policy for details.

4. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data you submitted via the contact form. To exercise these rights, email mukharbek.organokov@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request". I will respond within 30 days.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. The date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the contact form after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
